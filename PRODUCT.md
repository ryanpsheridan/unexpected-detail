## Platform

web

## Users

Primary, until the December 2026 launch: future listeners. People who hear
about the show (from Marcy, friends, social sneak previews) and land on the
site with no episodes to play yet. Their job: understand what the show is,
get curious, and join the list to hear the first episode.

Prospective guests are reached separately, through the guest invitation
one-pager. The concept and round pages are internal review pages for Marcy
and her team, not for the public.

## Product Purpose

Unexpected Detail is a conversation podcast hosted by Marcy about what rarely
makes the official biography: the detours, half-formed ideas, quiet
obsessions, and stories that make people who they are. The site sets the
tone before launch, collects launch sign-ups, and invites listeners to send
their own unexpected detail or a question for a guest. Success before
launch: sign-ups and submissions. After launch: a home for episodes.

## Positioning

Not an interview to get through: a warm, unscripted conversation that follows
the detour. Signature mechanism: the Handoff. Each guest answers a question
left by the previous guest and leaves one for someone they have never met.
Listeners take part too: "Everyone has one. What's yours?"

## Brand Commitments

- Taglines: "Curiosity flips the frame." and "Everyone has one. What's yours?"
  Openers: "Good questions. Great detours." and "Smart. Warm. A little
  offbeat."
- Voice: smart, warm, a little offbeat; genuinely curious; never markety.
- Do not use publicly: the conversation rhythm (the five beats, kept
  internal), the mission line "To rescue human conversation from the résumé",
  "Human beings are endlessly layered", "No chasing a dramatic reveal".
- Ryan dislikes the look of Kristen's deck and one-pager PDFs; they are a
  content source only, not a design reference. The visual authority is the
  concept page (`/concept`).

## Product Principles

1. Intrigue over announcement: no "coming soon" copy; make people curious.
2. People, not profiles: the show is about the person under the résumé.
3. Listeners take part: every page leaves a way to share a detail or question.
4. Protect the conversation: what makes the format work stays off the site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text must meet WCAG AA contrast. Ryan's explicit rule: no light gold (butter)
for text, on any ground.
